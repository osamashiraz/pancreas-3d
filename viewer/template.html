Region

  • Duct tree: a large interlobular duct in the septa, interlobular ducts to each lobule, intralobular ducts inside, terminal (intercalated) ducts ending in acini
  • Acini, in grape-like clusters at the ends of the terminal ducts (drawn sparser than real, so the ducts show)
  • Lobules cut across, purple as in H&E, with pale pink septa of connective tissue between them: the ring round the opening
  • Islets: pale islands among the acini, sizes about the mean of 110 µm, at the region’s density
  • Interlobular ducts, with an artery (red) and vein (blue), in the septa

Acinar cells reach out until they meet a neighbouring acinus, a vessel, a duct or the islet, so acini deform against one another; they fill 52% of each tested block. Sizes of acinar, centroacinar, duct and beta cells, acini, granules, capillaries and stellate cells are typical values not yet sourced; the duct tree and centroacinar cells are described in rat (Endo 2002).
