Pancreatic tissue

A 300 µm block from inside a lobule: ducts branching into acini, and an islet among them.

Simulated: built from published descriptions and measurements

  • Pyramidal, base on the outside of the acinus and apex at the lumen; 12 µm from base to apex and 10 µm across the base (not yet sourced). Grouped round a narrow central lumen in tube-shaped acini closed at one end, budding in grape-like clusters from the ducts (CODA, Kiemen 2022).

  • About 1 µm across, packed in the apical part of each acinar cell, where they release digestive enzymes into the lumen (Messenger 2014; measured in rat by Nadelhaft 1973 and Ermak and Rothman 1981). 24 are drawn per cell; a cell holds many more.

  • Acinar nuclei round and basal, 5.5 µm across (not yet sourced); the average nuclear radius in human pancreas sections is 2 µm across all cell types (CODA). Centroacinar nuclei are elongated, duct nuclei small.

  • Thin, pale, flattened cells, 3 per acinus, lining the intra-acinar duct: the start of the duct tree inside the acinus, where the acinar cells are shorter (Endo 2002, in rat). A specialised duct cell (Beer 2016). Size 12 × 6 × 2 µm (not yet sourced). Best seen in "One acinus".

  • Short, narrow ducts of low, flattened cells continuing the centroacinar cells; each serves one acinus and joins a larger duct. Acini never join one another (Endo 2002). Outer radius 5 µm (not yet sourced).

  • Cuboidal epithelium, branching through the lobule and tapering from 12 µm outer radius (not yet sourced); grown so every part of the block lies within reach of one.

  • The representative human islet, 110 µm across: beta (blue), alpha (red), delta (green) and PP (purple) cells intermixed, set among the acini without a duct of its own.

Rigid tube-shaped acini pack loosely: they fill 21% of this block, where real acini fill most of the lobule, pressing against one another. Stroma, capillaries and nerves between the acini are not drawn yet. Measurements marked not yet sourced are typical histology values awaiting a citation; descriptions from rat are marked so.
