# How the project got here (summary)

1. Organ: HRA reference pancreas regions; inventory with evidence levels and statuses; ontology copied from HRA with errors listed.
2. Extraction of ducts, vessels, duodenum and spleen from the HRA united models, bytes unchanged; loader through the bounded fetch.
3. Simulated vessels on landmark rules; grid shortest-path routing replaced point pushing after contacts and kinks; arcades close.
4. Splenic branches and lymph node stations with nearest-free placement.
5. Representative islet calibrated to Cabrera's 71% (random intermixing suffices once cells pack as tissue); islet distribution through the organ.
6. Tissue block: tube acini (21%) → rounded acini → overlap bug found (reported 66% was false) → fill pass → deformation by power diagram (52%).
7. Viewer: organ-to-cell zoom → continuous nested zoom → stages with hysteresis → structure alone, cells separated, one cell → duct branch → slice apart, names, picking, motion, journey → regions and gross level → 60 sites and generated blocks/islets → split screen, voice → 3D H&E with cut-face fill and stain grain → cell-type stain for all structures → professional voices, narration from StatPearls and histology sources, journey continues during interaction, cut open off by default. → lobule level shows the duct hierarchy (large, interlobular, intralobular, terminal ducts) with acinar clusters and scattered islets.
13. Slice apart fixed and extended: organ slices are solid with filled cut faces; the tissue core (not the whole block) slices across the view, each slab keeping the core's shape; a structure or a single cell can be sliced too. PanIN panel: progression in numbers (Ki-67 by grade, Klein et al. 2002; KRAS, MUC1 and cyclin D1 by grade, as reviewed in Koorstra et al. 2008).
12. Precursor views rebuilt as histology: real H&E micrographs (Hackeng 2016; Shibata 2017; Nephron and Humpath on Wikimedia Commons) with numbered features, what sets each lesion apart, and a PanIN/IPMN/MCN comparison table; the 3D lesion sketches were removed.
11. Modes (Pancreas; Precursor lesions; Pancreatitis and PDA to come); a simpler pancreas ladder (organ → tissue → exocrine and endocrine); precursor overview with insets linked to the organ as in Hingorani 2007 Figure 1; PanIN, IPMN and MCN views down to their cells; cards checked against Beger 4th ed. and Robbins 9th ed.
10. Part 3 begun: an organ-level map of precursors and cancer (PanIN, MCN, main- and branch-duct IPMN, ductal adenocarcinoma) placed by the location rules of Hingorani 2007, with information cards.
9. Part 1 of the next steps: smoke test in CI before each deployment, links to a view, names hidden behind the lobule wall, narration following the layers. Part 2: alpha and delta cells at the cell level (granule sizes from Brereton 2015, delta processes from Arrojo e Drigo 2019), secretion in motion for acinar, duct, beta, alpha and delta cells with journey narration, and a marker-gene panel from the HRA ASCT+B table and the Human Protein Atlas.
8. Layers instead of blocks: the 4 mm piece and the 300 µm block replaced by openings in place, after MouseBio_3D's zoom-unfold. The organ opens round the line going in at a site, the lobules inside stand cut across with an opening onto their duct tree, and that opens onto a core of the tested block; each layer stays as a ring round the next. The solid-lobule switch and the block cut slider were removed; the comparison shows three opened columns.
