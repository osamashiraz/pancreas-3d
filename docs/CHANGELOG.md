# How the project got here (summary)

1. Organ: HRA reference pancreas regions; inventory with evidence levels and statuses; ontology copied from HRA with errors listed.
2. Extraction of ducts, vessels, duodenum and spleen from the HRA united models, bytes unchanged; loader through the bounded fetch.
3. Simulated vessels on landmark rules; grid shortest-path routing replaced point pushing after contacts and kinks; arcades close.
4. Splenic branches and lymph node stations with nearest-free placement.
5. Representative islet calibrated to Cabrera's 71% (random intermixing suffices once cells pack as tissue); islet distribution through the organ.
6. Tissue block: tube acini (21%) → rounded acini → overlap bug found (reported 66% was false) → fill pass → deformation by power diagram (52%).
7. Viewer: organ-to-cell zoom → continuous nested zoom → stages with hysteresis → structure alone, cells separated, one cell → duct branch → slice apart, names, picking, motion, journey → regions and gross level → 60 sites and generated blocks/islets → split screen, voice → 3D H&E with cut-face fill and stain grain → cell-type stain for all structures → professional voices, narration from StatPearls and histology sources, journey continues during interaction, cut open off by default. → lobule level shows the duct hierarchy (large, interlobular, intralobular, terminal ducts) with acinar clusters and scattered islets.
22. Faster rendering, measured with a new profiler (viewer/test/profile.py: draw calls, triangles and frame times at each stage, optionally at 2× pixel density). The tissue core was drawing the whole 300 µm block and discarding most of it in the shader: the block now keeps only what can lie in the core (Tissue level 9.6 M → 2.3 M triangles; 13 fps before, now 45–60 fps depending on the site, also at 2×). Gross acini and the block's duct cells use lighter shapes (Lobules 2.1 M → 0.7 M triangles). On dense (Retina) screens multisampling is off, and a single cell no longer draws its islet at 5% behind it unless "Show the surrounding tissue" is on: beta cell 29 → about 58 fps, duct cell 52 → 60 fps at 2×. Dynamic resolution lowers the pixel ratio (never below 1×) while frames run slow and raises it again when there is room. Dead code (old weights, anchors and levels, an unused helper, unused text) removed. Insets now use canonical high-power H&E, all CC BY 4.0 unless noted: PanIN-2 duct (Distler 2014), low-grade IPMN papillae (Chen 2023), MCN wall ×40 (Distler 2014), PDA ×400 (Aloraini 2023), acute fat necrosis (Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0) and, for the first time, a real micrograph of chronic pancreatitis (Chekmareva 2025); acinar necrosis ×200 (Mihoc 2024) and high-grade IPMN added to the panels.
21. Panel tidied: sources and citations folded (closed by default) in the side panel, marker box, lesion cards and histology panels; voice, speed, split screen, slice apart and the head/body/tail comparison under "More options"; the islet point cloud ("Show the islets") removed; a "Show insets" switch. High-power H&E (objective ×20 to ×40) of PanIN-1A, -2 and -3, gastric, intestinal and pancreatobiliary IPMN and the MCN wall from Distler et al. 2014 (CC BY) added to the panels with numbered pins; insets now show high-power views (PanIN-3, pancreatobiliary IPMN, MCN wall, PDA glands, acute fat necrosis). No openly licensed high-power H&E of chronic pancreatitis was found, so its inset shows the drawing.
20. Names on hover: resting the pointer on the organ, a vessel, a lesion, a lobule or duct, a cell or an organelle names it in a box below the level's name (top right). The organs round the pancreas are off by default.
19. Pancreatitis and PDA modes (organ changes, drawings, Commons micrographs, cards, comparison tables), and the organs round the pancreas (liver, gallbladder, kidneys, great vessels, transverse colon) extracted unchanged from the HRA body model.
18. Papers added by the user read in full: the Japan Pancreas Society eighth edition verifies the lymph node stations (reference marked verified; module tests updated and passing, 23/23); Furukawa 2005 confirms the IPMN types as drawn; Brereton 2015 settles delta granule size (250 nm, smaller than alpha and beta) and adds delta-cell processes up to 22 µm.
17. Drawings revised from a review of the histology literature (Basturk 2015, Distler 2014, Brune 2006, Guo 2016, Assarzadegan 2021, Adsay 2016, Matthaei 2011, Malaspina 2025; Hruban 2001/2004, Furukawa 2005 and Zamboni 1999 were not accessible beyond abstracts): PanIN with lobular atrophy and two-tier grade labels; IPMN with intestinal, pancreatobiliary and gastric types (goblet cells, elongated nuclei, foveolar cells, pyloric-type glands); MCN with varied and denuded lining, luteinized cells, hyalinized areas and a calcified capsule. README no longer calls the project a MouseBio_3D module.
16. Tissue sites: thirty, scattered at random, each showing its region's tested block (no more generation in the browser, which froze the page); uncinate-process sites get a PP-rich islet (Gorelick and Williams 2021).
15. IPMN and MCN drawings in the same H&E style (dilated main duct with finger-like mucinous papillae and a complex papilla; branch-duct cysts opening into the duct; MCN capsule, dense wavy ovarian-type stroma, mucinous lining, septum, unconnected duct). Each panel shows the drawing first, then the real H&E with the same numbers, then links to more real examples at Johns Hopkins (not reproduced: their images are not openly licensed).
14. Histology panels: numbered pins only (the text is in the list below, and on hover); PanIN gets a drawing in H&E colours (normal duct, PanIN-1A, 1B, 2, 3 among acini and stroma, with the genetic changes), shown first, with the micrograph one click away; PanIN and IPMN insets swapped.
13. Slice apart fixed and extended: organ slices are solid with filled cut faces; the tissue core (not the whole block) slices across the view, each slab keeping the core's shape; a structure or a single cell can be sliced too. PanIN panel: progression in numbers (Ki-67 by grade, Klein et al. 2002; KRAS, MUC1 and cyclin D1 by grade, as reviewed in Koorstra et al. 2008).
12. Precursor views rebuilt as histology: real H&E micrographs (Hackeng 2016; Shibata 2017; Nephron and Humpath on Wikimedia Commons) with numbered features, what sets each lesion apart, and a PanIN/IPMN/MCN comparison table; the 3D lesion sketches were removed.
11. Modes (Pancreas; Precursor lesions; Pancreatitis and PDA to come); a simpler pancreas ladder (organ → tissue → exocrine and endocrine); precursor overview with insets linked to the organ as in Hingorani 2007 Figure 1; PanIN, IPMN and MCN views down to their cells; cards checked against Beger 4th ed. and Robbins 9th ed.
10. Part 3 begun: an organ-level map of precursors and cancer (PanIN, MCN, main- and branch-duct IPMN, ductal adenocarcinoma) placed by the location rules of Hingorani 2007, with information cards.
9. Part 1 of the next steps: smoke test in CI before each deployment, links to a view, names hidden behind the lobule wall, narration following the layers. Part 2: alpha and delta cells at the cell level (granule sizes from Brereton 2015, delta processes from Arrojo e Drigo 2019), secretion in motion for acinar, duct, beta, alpha and delta cells with journey narration, and a marker-gene panel from the HRA ASCT+B table and the Human Protein Atlas.
8. Layers instead of blocks: the 4 mm piece and the 300 µm block replaced by openings in place, after MouseBio_3D's zoom-unfold. The organ opens round the line going in at a site, the lobules inside stand cut across with an opening onto their duct tree, and that opens onto a core of the tested block; each layer stays as a ring round the next. The solid-lobule switch and the block cut slider were removed; the comparison shows three opened columns.
