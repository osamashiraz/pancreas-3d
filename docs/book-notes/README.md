# Book notes

Paraphrased facts, with page references, taken from the books kept locally in `literature/` (not in this repository). These notes list what each book says and where, so that values in the model can be checked against the printed editions. They are in our own words; the books themselves are not reproduced.

- `normal_report.md`, `normal-facts.json`: normal anatomy and histology (Gorelick & Williams 2021; Beger et al. 4th ed. 2023; Golson 2024; Robbins 9th ed.).
- `lesions_report.md`, `lesion-facts.json`: precursor lesions, cancer, pancreatitis and diabetes (Robbins 9th ed.; Beger et al. 4th ed.; Golson 2024; Hingorani 2007).

- `atlas-facts.json`: 281 facts for the atlas's entries (definition, architecture, cells, nuclei, mucin, stroma, size, epidemiology, markers, outlook, look-alikes), each with source and page (Hruban 2001; Kardon 2001; Basturk 2015; Kleeff 2016; Robbins 9th ed. ch. 19 and 24; Beger et al. 4th ed.; Gorelick and Williams 2021 ch. 1).

Page numbers are given as PDF page / printed page. Extracted with `pdftotext -layout` and searched by script; two facts were spot-checked against the text (PanIN 82% with PDAC, Beger p969; main-duct IPMN 43.6% invasive, Beger p972).
